Families and co-ops

For families and co-ops

Griffin & Quill is built for homeschool families and co-ops first, then parents and teachers. A note on this page is a question for the studio. It is not a purchase, a quote, or a booking.

Where to start

Tutoring and the store

Tutoring is open now: $40 per hour online and $50 per hour in person. Classroom units are listed in the store and purchased on Teachers Pay Teachers. This site does not take payment.

Co-op workshops

A workshop for the adults in a homeschool co-op is a question, not a posted calendar. Topics we can plan: academic skills across subjects, the learn-practice-prove routine, and teaching from a Griffin & Quill lesson.

What helps us answer

  • Whether you are asking about tutoring, a unit, Quillhaven, or a co-op workshop.
  • Who the learners are: a family, a co-op, or another group of adults.
  • Roughly how many adults or learners, if you know.
  • Whether a workshop would be remote or in person. We may not be able to travel. We will say so.

Quillhaven, Forge, and Command Center are described on the Quillhaven page. They are not something you can buy from this form.
