Schools and districts

For schools and districts

Two conversations, neither of them a checkout. Site licenses for Quillhaven, when that town can be played, and professional development workshops for teachers.

Send an inquiry

What you can ask about

Coming soon

Site licenses

A license so more than one classroom can use Quillhaven, at a school or across a district. The town is not for sale yet. An inquiry now is a note for later, not an order.

PD workshops

A session for teachers, or for a homeschool co-op that wants a shared workshop. Topics we can plan: reading sources, writing a claim with evidence, and teaching from a Griffin & Quill lesson. There is no public calendar.

What helps us answer

  • Whether you want a license, a workshop, or both.
  • Who the audience is: one school, a district, or a co-op.
  • Roughly how many teachers or students, if you know.
  • Whether you want the workshop remote or in person. We may not be able to travel. We will say so.

Teachers can still buy single resources on Teachers Pay Teachers when that store is open. A district-wide set is a separate conversation.
