Tutoring

Is tutoring online?

Yes. Tutoring is online first. You meet by video. If you need a different setup, say so on the consult form. We will tell you if we can do it.

What subjects do you cover?

U.S. History, Government, Economics, and AP Human Geography. Sessions can also focus on world history, study skills, and test prep for those courses.

Who is tutoring for?

Parents, homeschoolers, and students, including adults who want to study for themselves. If you are a student, use a parent or guardian email on the form.

How does a new student start?

Three steps. A free consult, then a written plan, then sessions. The consult does not lock you into a paid plan.

Do you tutor small groups?

Yes. A small-group seat is $25 per student for a 60-minute online session. A group is 2 to 4 students.

How much does tutoring cost?

The published rates are on the tutoring page. A 60-minute intro session is $35, one per family. A 60-minute 1:1 session is $50. A 45-minute middle school session is $40. A 90-minute essay and DBQ intensive is $75. A 4-session pack is $180. An 8-session pack is $340. A small-group seat is $25. The USHC EOC boot camp is $125. The AP Human Geography boot camp is $150. Shop

Where do I buy the classroom resources?

On Teachers Pay Teachers, when the store is open. Buttons on this site are placeholder links. They do not open a store yet.

Can a homeschooler use the resources?

Yes. The resources are for teachers and homeschoolers.

Is there a free sample?

A free sample is planned on the TPT store. The button on the shop page is a placeholder.

Quillhaven

Can I play Quillhaven now?

Not yet. The Quillhaven page has a town, a reserved slot for a pixel-art logo, and placeholder scenes. You can join a waitlist. On this mockup the form does not send. A demo is coming later.

What is the art style?

The Quillhaven page uses a pixel-art accent. The final illustrations, including Tahne, Merlin, and Winston Wolf, are not on this site yet. The griffin-and-quill mark in the header is the final logo.

Do the kids’ games collect personal data?

The kids’ games are designed to collect no personal data. No account, no name, and no tracking inside a game. That is a COPPA-minded choice. The Privacy Policy is still a draft and needs an attorney.

Schools and districts

Can a school license Quillhaven?

Site licenses are the plan, and they are not for sale yet. The schools page has an inquiry form. This mockup does not send it.

Do you offer workshops?

Workshops are an inquiry, not a posted calendar. They can cover reading sources, writing a claim with evidence, and using a Griffin & Quill lesson.
