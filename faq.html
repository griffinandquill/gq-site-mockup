Tutoring

Is tutoring online?

Yes. Tutoring is online first. You meet by video. If you need a different setup, say so when you book. We will tell you if we can do it.

What subjects do you cover?

Sessions build academic skills in whatever course a student is taking. Ryan Mayer teaches U.S. History, Government, Economics, and AP Human Geography, and sessions can also focus on study skills and test prep.

Who is tutoring for?

Parents, homeschool families, and students, including adults who want to study for themselves. A student should book through a parent or guardian.

How does a new student start?

Three steps. A skills check, then a written plan, then sessions. The first session starts with a quick skills check.

How much does tutoring cost?

One-to-one online tutoring is a flat $50 per hour. Book with Griffin & Quill first. Wyzant is also available. This site does not take payment.

Store

Where do I find the units?

Open the store. Griffin & Quill is a Teachers Pay Teachers Exclusive Seller, so Buy on TPT opens that product in a new tab. This site does not take payment. A subject that is still in progress says so and links to the TPT store.

Who is the store for?

Homeschool families, co-ops, and teachers.

Quillhaven

What is Quillhaven?

A subject-neutral dungeon crawler RPG that teaches, trains and tests academic skills. Dungeon runs unlock pages of the Great Book; each page opens a Chapter Guardian, a replayable, tiered challenge that tests one skill and awards a medal. Students also earn titles and items as they grow.

Can I play Quillhaven now?

Yes. On the Quillhaven page, press Play the demo. The game opens in a new tab and goes full screen when you tap to play. It plays best on a computer or tablet with a keyboard. You can still join the waitlist.

What is the art style?

Quillhaven is drawn in pixel art. The griffin-and-quill mark in the header is the company logo.

What does Quillhaven collect?

Quillhaven collects only what learning requires, never sells it, and has no ads or trackers. The Privacy Policy is still a draft and needs an attorney.

Schools and districts

Can a school license Quillhaven?

Schools and districts can ask about a license for Quillhaven and for Griffin & Quill units. Licenses are not for sale yet. The schools page has an inquiry form. That note goes to partners@griffinandquill.com.

Do you offer workshops?

Workshops are an inquiry, not a posted calendar. They can cover building academic skills across subjects, the learn-practice-prove routine, and teaching from a Griffin & Quill lesson.
