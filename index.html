How it works

The same three moves show up in a tutoring session, a lesson, and a street in Quillhaven.

  1. 1

    Play

    Step into the question

    A source, a map, a choice, or a scene with something at stake. The student has to notice before they are told.

  2. 2

    Learn

    Learn the hard part

    A South Carolina teacher walks the sticking point: the term, the disagreement, or the sentence that will not come out.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Say it back

    The student states a claim, points at the evidence, and says why it holds. That is the legend they get to keep.
