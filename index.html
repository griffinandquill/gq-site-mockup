How it works

The same habit shows up in a tutoring session, a lesson, and a run through Quillhaven: learn it, practice it, then prove it. Forge and the Command Center use that habit too.

  1. 1

    Learn

    Walk through the hard part

    A South Carolina teacher walks through the hard part, the idea or the step that does not land yet.

  2. 2

    Practice

    Work the skill

    The student works the skill: guided at first, then on their own, with rewards that keep it sharp.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Show the skill

    The student shows the skill on something new. In Quillhaven, that is the Chapter Guardian. That is the legend they get to keep.

Built by a South Carolina teacher

Ryan Mayer is a South Carolina licensed, ESOL-licensed teacher with more than seven years in the classroom and a Master’s in History Education. He teaches U.S. History, Government, Economics, and AP Human Geography.

Griffin & Quill LLC is his South Carolina company. One login and one subscription covers Quillhaven, Quillhaven Forge, and the Command Center. Tutoring and the store sit beside that. The habit is the same: learn it, then prove it.
