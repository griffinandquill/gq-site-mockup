Quillhaven

A subject-neutral dungeon crawler RPG that teaches, trains and tests academic skills. How it works

The same habit shows up in a tutoring session, a lesson, and a run through Quillhaven: learn it, practice it, then prove it.

  1. 1

    Learn

    Walk through the hard part

    A South Carolina teacher walks through the hard part, the idea or the step that does not land yet.

  2. 2

    Practice

    Work the skill

    The student works the skill: guided at first, then on their own, with rewards that keep it sharp.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Show the skill

    The student shows the skill on something new. In Quillhaven, that is the Chapter Guardian. That is the legend they get to keep.

Built by a South Carolina teacher

Ryan Mayer is a South Carolina licensed, ESOL-licensed teacher in the Beaufort County School District. He previously taught with the New York City Department of Education, with more than seven years in the classroom.

Griffin & Quill LLC is his South Carolina company. It teaches, grows, and maintains students’ academic skills across all subjects through tutoring, curriculum, and Quillhaven, around one habit: learn it, then prove it.

The work is meant to sit beside the standards he teaches. When a card names a standard, that standard is marked as a sample until the real one is confirmed.
