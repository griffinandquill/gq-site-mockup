Three parts, one plan

Griffin & Quill is for homeschool families and co-ops first, then parents and teachers. Quillhaven, Quillhaven Forge, and Command Center are one ecosystem. A single login is planned later. It is not offered here, and this site does not sell a subscription.

Demo planned

Quillhaven

Gamified practice for academic skills. A demo is planned for December 2, 2026. The current rebuild is TypeScript and Phaser 4. This site does not launch the game.

Planned

Quillhaven Forge

A simulation maker for any subject. An adult chooses a topic and an academic skill. Fixed templates, and an adult approves what a student sees. An MVP is planned for spring 2027. The chart above is a sample story, not Forge.

Tutoring
