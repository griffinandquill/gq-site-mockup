About

A South Carolina studio for learning you can prove.

Griffin & Quill LLC is an edtech company in South Carolina. We help people learn something they can say out loud: what happened, why people disagreed, and which sources support a claim.

Mission

Write your own legend. Learn it. Live it. PROVE IT!

Where learning becomes an adventure.

Griffin & Quill builds standards-aligned games and lessons where students play through the story, reason with real evidence, and prove what they learned, all designed by a working teacher to fit a real class period.

Founder

Ryan Mayer is a South Carolina high school social studies teacher. His social studies courses are U.S. History and the Constitution (USHC), Economics, AP Human Geography, and Government. He has taught social studies for more than seven years and holds a Master’s in History Education. He founded Griffin & Quill LLC.

In his own words, history too often feels like names and dates to memorize. He wants students to feel like they are stepping inside the story.

Values

Accuracy

Names, dates, maps, and quotations get checked before a lesson or a scene ships. If a fact is disputed, the material says so.

Fairness to more than one view

When people in the past disagreed, we show more than one sourced view. A student can argue a view, and can change their mind when the evidence asks them to.

Accessibility

Clear language, keyboard use, and readable type on this site. Quillhaven will need captions and a way to play without a mouse. We are building toward WCAG AA. The games are not built yet, so this is not a finished audit.
