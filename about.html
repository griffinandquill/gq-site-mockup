About

Where learning becomes an adventure.

Griffin & Quill teaches, grows, and maintains students’ academic skills across all subjects through tutoring, curriculum, and Quillhaven.

Mission

Griffin & Quill teaches, grows, and maintains students’ academic skills across all subjects through tutoring, curriculum, and Quillhaven, each built by a practicing teacher around one habit: learn it, then prove it.

Quillhaven is a subject-neutral dungeon crawler that teaches, trains, and tests those skills. Its line is: Write your own legend. Learn it. Live it. PROVE IT!

Vision

One adventure where every academic skill is a chapter to master, every challenge ends with proof of learning, and every student carries those skills into every subject.

Founder

Ryan Mayer is a South Carolina licensed teacher in the Beaufort County School District, where he teaches U.S. History, Government, Economics, and AP Human Geography. He previously taught with the New York City Department of Education, is ESOL licensed, and holds a Master’s in History Education, with more than seven years in the classroom.

He founded Griffin & Quill LLC.

Values

Mastery over memorization

We build skills students can use on anything new, not facts they repeat once and forget.

Practice, then prove it

Every lesson, session, and run moves from guided practice to a clear demonstration of the skill.

Play with purpose

Every game mechanic, reward, and challenge exists to serve learning. Quillhaven will need captions and a way to play without a mouse. We are building toward WCAG AA. The games are not built yet, so this is not a finished audit.

Durable retention means skills are revisited, reinforced, and rewarded over time so they last beyond the unit or the test. Teacher-built rigor means a practicing teacher designs every product to be demanding, accurate, original, and trustworthy for families and schools.
