Quillhaven

Where knowledge is power and every street holds a new adventure.

A fantasy town from Griffin & Quill. You walk in, you learn something that matters on that street, and you prove you can carry it back out.

The town

These pixel figures are stand-ins, not final character art. The pixel-art Quillhaven logo will replace the reserved slot above.

Tahne

Tahne, a gryphon, founded Quillhaven. The town runs on what its people can explain, not on what they can hoard.

Merlin

Merlin keeps the doors at Quillhaven Academy. Each portal opens on a subject. He does not hand you the answer. He hands you the way in.

Winston Wolf

Winston Wolf walks the night watch. When the town is quiet, he asks whether you can still prove what you learned that day.
