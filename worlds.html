The town

Tahne founded the town. Merlin keeps the Academy portals. Professor Bramble keeps the Library. Winston Wolf keeps the Map Room.

Tahne

Tahne is the gryphon who founded Quillhaven. He is wise, brave, and encouraging: a guide and a guardian of knowledge, not a monster. He holds a quill, and he greets new heroes in the town plaza.

Merlin

Merlin is the wizard who keeps the Academy portals. Each portal opens a subject hub. He does not hand you the answer. He hands you the way in.

Winston Wolf

Winston Wolf keeps the Map Room. He asks whether you can still prove what you learned that day.

Professor Bramble

Professor Bramble keeps the Library. He is the highland cow librarian.

Quillhaven Academy

Quillhaven Academy sits at the heart of town. Four subject portals each open a hub world. The games live inside the hub.
