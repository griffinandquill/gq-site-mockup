The town

Tahne, Merlin, Professor Bramble, and Winston Wolf keep Quillhaven. Professor Bramble is the highland cow librarian. The pixel-art town logo is still reserved.

Tahne

Tahne is the gryphon who founded Quillhaven. He is wise, brave, and encouraging: a guide and a guardian of knowledge, not a monster. He holds a quill, and he greets new heroes in the town plaza.

Merlin

Merlin is the wizard who keeps the Academy portals. Each portal opens on a subject. He does not hand you the answer. He hands you the way in.

Winston Wolf

Winston Wolf is the night watchman. Lantern in hand, he keeps watch over Quillhaven after dark and asks whether you can still prove what you learned that day.

Quillhaven Academy

Quillhaven Academy sits at the heart of town. Magic portals lead to subject worlds. Social studies is the first portal, because that is Ryan’s specialty. The plan is to open a portal for every subject. These four line up with the courses Griffin & Quill tutors.
